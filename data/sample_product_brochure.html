Product Summary

The ABC Investment Product is a diversified medium-risk unit trust offering exposure to a broad range of global equities and fixed-income instruments. It is designed for investors seeking capital growth over a 5 to 10 year horizon.

Key Features

  • Diversified portfolio across global equities and bonds
  • Professionally managed by the bank's Investment Committee
  • Daily liquidity — redemptions processed on the next business day
  • Available as a lump sum or regular contribution plan
  • ISA-eligible — can be held within a Stocks and Shares ISA wrapper
  • Online account access and quarterly performance statements

Charges

Charge Type Rate
Annual Management Charge 0.75% per annum
Initial Charge 0% (no initial charge)
Early Exit Fee (within 12 months) 2.00%
Ongoing Charges Figure (OCF) 0.90% per annum

Risk Warning

The value of investments can fall as well as rise. You may get back less than you invest. Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results. Capital is at risk.

Suitability

This product is suitable for investors who:

  • Have a medium to high risk tolerance
  • Can commit to a minimum 5-year investment horizon
  • Are seeking capital growth rather than income
  • Understand that capital is at risk

How to Apply

Applications can be submitted via the Relationship Manager portal. A completed application form and proof of identity are required. Minimum investment is $10,000.
