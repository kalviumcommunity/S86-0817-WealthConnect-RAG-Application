Eligibility Guidelines — ABC Investment Product
Version: 2.1
Approval Status: Approved
Effective Date: 01/01/2024
Owner: Wealth Team

1. Customer Eligibility

The following eligibility criteria must be verified before proceeding
with an application for the ABC Investment Product:

1.1 Age
- The customer must be at least 18 years of age.
- There is no maximum age restriction.

1.2 Residency
- The customer must be a resident in a supported jurisdiction.
- Non-residents are not eligible without prior approval from Compliance.

1.3 Risk Profile
- The customer must have a verified medium to high risk tolerance.
- A completed Risk Profile Assessment (RPA) must be on file before application.
- Customers with a low or cautious risk profile are not eligible for this product.

1.4 Know Your Customer (KYC)
- Standard KYC documentation must be completed and verified.
- Enhanced due diligence (EDD) is required for investments exceeding $500,000.

2. Investment Limits

2.1 Minimum Investment
- Lump sum: $10,000
- Regular contribution: $500 per month

2.2 Maximum Investment
- Up to $1,000,000 per calendar year under standard KYC.
- Amounts exceeding $1,000,000 require enhanced KYC and Compliance sign-off.

3. Unsuitable Customer Profiles

This product is NOT suitable for customers who:
- Require guaranteed capital protection
- Cannot afford to lose any of the invested amount
- Have a short-term investment horizon of less than 5 years
- Cannot tolerate fluctuations in the value of their investment

4. Relationship Manager Responsibility

Relationship Managers must confirm all eligibility criteria are met
before submitting an application. Applications submitted without a
verified Risk Profile Assessment will be rejected.

All eligibility decisions must be documented in the customer record.
