Investment Policy — ABC Investment Product
Version: 3.2
Approval Status: Approved
Effective Date: 01/01/2024
Owner: Wealth Team

1. Overview
The ABC Investment Product is a medium-risk unit trust designed for investors
seeking capital growth over a 5 to 10 year investment horizon.

2. Eligibility Requirements
- Minimum age: 18 years
- Minimum investment: $10,000
- Residency: Available to residents of supported jurisdictions only
- Risk profile: Medium to high

3. Investment Limits
- Minimum lump sum: $10,000
- Maximum lump sum: $1,000,000 per calendar year without additional KYC
- Regular contributions: Minimum $500 per month

4. Key Risks
- Capital at risk — the value of the investment can fall as well as rise
- Liquidity risk — early redemption within 12 months incurs a penalty
- Market risk — performance is linked to underlying fund performance
- Currency risk — some underlying assets are denominated in foreign currencies

5. Early Redemption
Redemption within the first 12 months is subject to a 2% early exit fee.
Redemption after 12 months carries no exit fee.

6. Governance
This policy is reviewed annually by the Wealth Division and the Compliance Team.
The current approved version supersedes all previous versions.
