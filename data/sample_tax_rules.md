## 1. Capital Gains Tax

Returns on the ABC Investment Product above the annual Capital Gains Tax (CGT)
exemption threshold are subject to CGT at the investor's applicable rate.

- **Basic rate taxpayers:** 10% on gains above the annual exemption
- **Higher rate taxpayers:** 20% on gains above the annual exemption
- **Annual CGT exemption:** Subject to current HMRC guidelines (review annually)

---

## 2. Income Tax on Interest

Interest income generated by the product is taxed at the investor's marginal
income tax rate.

- Interest is reported annually on the investor's tax statement
- The bank issues a consolidated tax certificate each April

---

## 3. Long-Term Capital Gains Rate

Investments held for more than **12 months** qualify for the reduced long-term
capital gains rate where applicable under current legislation.

Investments redeemed within **12 months** are subject to standard income tax
rates on any gains realised.

---

## 4. Tax-Free Allowances

Certain tax-free allowances may apply subject to current HMRC guidelines:

- **ISA wrapper:** If held within a Stocks and Shares ISA, returns are
  sheltered from CGT and income tax up to the annual ISA allowance.
- **Personal Savings Allowance:** Interest below the Personal Savings Allowance
  threshold is not subject to income tax.

---

## 5. Important Notice

Tax treatment depends on individual circumstances and may change.
Relationship Managers must refer customers to an independent tax adviser
for personal tax guidance. This document does not constitute tax advice.
