=== Task 4: Justification ===
For this financial RAG application (WealthConnect), **Strategy 2 (Paragraph Chunking)** is the chosen strategy.
Reasoning: Financial documents (like earnings reports and account summaries) are highly structured by topic per paragraph. Using fixed-size chunking blindly slices through sentences and numbers (e.g., cutting '$10,000' in half), destroying the factual integrity needed for accurate retrieval. Paragraph chunking respects the natural semantic boundaries, ensuring that concepts remain whole and contextually complete when fed to the LLM.
